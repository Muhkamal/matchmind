Football predictions you can audit

Every pick is written to an append-only ledger before kickoff and graded automatically, including the misses. Probabilities, not promises.

What do these numbers mean?
  • Graded live picks: predictions locked before kickoff whose match has finished. Only these count.
  • Live accuracy: how often the most likely result happened. Easy to read, but it ignores how confident the pick was.
  • Brier score: penalises confident wrong picks more than cautious ones. Lower is better. Always guessing 1/3 each scores 0.667.
  • Small samples are noise. A handful of picks cannot show whether the model is good. Judge it over hundreds.
  • Goal markets (Over/Under 2.5, BTTS) are experimental: in backtests they do not reliably beat simple averages.
